Delete your account

Request deletion of your Tee Up Golf Society account and associated online data

Tee Up Golf Society is the new name of TeeSociety. The same operator, account service and privacy protections apply to both names.

You do not need to reinstall the app. Send the request from the email address used for your Tee Up Golf Society account so we can verify it belongs to you.

Request account deletion

Where available in your app version, sign in and open Account & backup > Request account deletion. Confirm your password to record the request in the app. Processing is manual, takes up to 30 days and is confirmed by email. Your local device data is kept. You can also use the email method below without reinstalling or signing in to the app.

  1. Select the button below to open an email addressed to Aspirewebdesign.
  2. Send the message from your registered Tee Up Golf Society email address.
  3. We may ask for limited additional information if needed to verify the request.
  4. We will confirm when the deletion is complete, normally sooner than one month.
Email a deletion request

If the button does not open your email app, write to contact@aspireweb.co.uk with the subject Tee Up Golf Society account deletion request.

What we delete

  • your Tee Up Golf Society authentication account and email address;
  • private cloud backups owned by your account;
  • your membership links and scorer assignments; and
  • other personal information linked to the account where deletion is technically and legally possible.

Some shared event, score or course records may be retained or anonymised where necessary to preserve records for other participants, prevent fraud, resolve disputes or meet legal obligations. Information that must be retained is protected and deleted when it is no longer required.

Data stored on your phone

Deleting the online account does not automatically erase golf data stored locally on every phone or tablet. To remove local data, first export anything you want to keep, then clear Tee Up Golf Society's app storage or uninstall the app from each device.

Delete only a cloud backup

You can remove a private backup without deleting your account: open More > Account, sign-in & cloud backup > Find my cloud backups, select the backup, and confirm deletion.
