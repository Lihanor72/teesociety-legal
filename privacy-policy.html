Privacy policy

Last updated 27 October 2026

Tee Up Golf Society is the new name of TeeSociety. The same operator, account service and privacy protections apply to both names.

Privacy in brief: Tee Up Golf Society works offline and keeps ordinary golf data on your device. Online features are optional. We do not sell personal information, show advertising or use advertising trackers. Scorecard photos are read on the device and are not uploaded by Tee Up Golf Society.

1. Who is responsible for your information

Tee Up Golf Society is operated by Harry Norrie, trading as Aspirewebdesign ("we", "us" or "our"). Harry Norrie is the controller of personal information processed through Tee Up Golf Society's online services.

Our correspondence address is 14 Franklin Avenue, Falkirk, FK2 7FU. For privacy questions or requests, email contact@aspireweb.co.uk. Our website is www.aspireweb.co.uk.

3. Information kept only on your device

By default, Tee Up Golf Society stores your profile, players, courses, events, leagues and scorecards in local app storage. This information is not sent to us unless you use an online feature, such as cloud backup, shared courses, shared leagues or live scoring.

Exported JSON backups and CSV results are created and shared only when you choose to export them. You control where an exported file or message is sent.

4. Camera and scorecard scanning

Tee Up Golf Society asks for camera or photo-library access only when you choose to scan a scorecard. Optical character recognition is performed on the device. Tee Up Golf Society does not upload the selected scorecard photo to our servers or retain a server copy. Parsed course details are shown for you to review before saving or publishing.

5. Why we use information

We use personal information to:

  • create and secure accounts;
  • provide optional backups and restoration;
  • publish and display courses, leagues, events, groups and scores when you request it;
  • prevent abuse, investigate faults and protect the service; and
  • answer support, privacy and deletion requests.

Under UK data-protection law, we generally rely on performance of our contract with you to provide requested account and connected features, and our legitimate interests in operating the service securely, preventing abuse and maintaining reliable shared golf records. Optional device permissions and cloud backup remain under your control.

6. Where information comes from

Most information comes directly from you. A golf organiser or another participant may also enter your name, handicap, group and scores when arranging or scoring a society event. Connected service providers generate account, security and backup metadata when those features are used.

7. Who can see information

People in the same shared league or live event can see the relevant roster, display names, handicaps, groups and scores. Community course details can be found by other Tee Up Golf Society users. Do not publish another person's information unless you have their permission or another lawful reason to do so.

We use Supabase to provide account authentication, database, backup and realtime services. Supabase processes this information for us under its data-processing terms and may use infrastructure subprocessors. Google Play or Apple may separately process store, installation and diagnostic information under their own privacy policies.

Optional pre-built course searches use GolfCourseAPI. We send the course search text or selected course identifier, not your players, handicaps, scorecards or account credentials. Avoid including personal information in a course search.

These public support and policy pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical visit information, including IP addresses, to serve and secure the website. See the GitHub privacy statement.

We do not sell personal information and do not provide it to advertising networks. We may disclose information where required by law or where necessary to protect users, our rights or the security of the service.

8. International processing

Service providers may process information outside the United Kingdom. Where UK data-protection law requires it, we rely on appropriate safeguards used by those providers, such as recognised adequacy arrangements or approved contractual protections.

9. How long information is kept

  • Local app data remains on your device until you delete it, clear the app's storage or uninstall the app.
  • Your account and private cloud backups are kept while your account remains active, unless you delete individual backups sooner.
  • Shared league, course and live-scoring records are kept while needed to provide the shared feature and preserve legitimate event records. When an account is deleted, we delete or anonymise linked personal information where reasonably possible, subject to technical, legal and security requirements.
  • Support and deletion correspondence is kept only as long as reasonably needed to resolve the request and meet legal obligations.

10. Security

We use access controls, authenticated connections and database row-level security to limit online information to authorised users. No system is completely secure, so protect your password and avoid sharing group or league access codes publicly.

11. Your choices and rights

You can use core scoring without an account, keep cloud backup disabled, remove individual cloud backups, and decline camera or photo access. Depending on the law that applies, you may ask for access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability or objection to our use of your personal information.

To delete your account and associated online data, follow our account deletion instructions. You can also email contact@aspireweb.co.uk.

Your right to object

You can object to processing based on our legitimate interests. Email us and explain your circumstances. We will stop unless we have compelling legitimate grounds to continue or the information is needed for legal claims.

If you are unhappy with our response, you may complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint.

12. Automated decisions

Tee Up Golf Society calculates handicaps, scores and rankings from the golf information entered, but we do not use personal information to make solely automated decisions that have legal or similarly significant effects.

13. Children

Tee Up Golf Society is intended for golf society organisers and players, not for children to use independently. Organisers must have an appropriate basis before entering information about a child and should limit it to what is necessary.

14. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when Tee Up Golf Society or its legal obligations change. We will publish the revised policy here and change the effective date. Material changes may also be highlighted in the app.
